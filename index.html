Phase 01 — The Opportunity Model

Our moat: one student, many opportunities

A prospective student is never just one lead for one course. Over a few weeks we approach the same person about several universities — Manipal, then Shoolini, and more. The decision that defines our degree sales is treating that person as one contact with many opportunities, not a fresh lead each time. It is what lets us nurture across programs, keep every past conversation, and never double-message — and the entire WhatsApp integration is built to protect it.

That raises one practical question: if a person is a single phone number — and so a single WhatsApp chat — how do many opportunities live on it? There are two ways, and they trade different problems against each other.

Model A · Keep it in one chat

One Counselling by HeroEd number carries every program for the student.

+ one coherent relationship+ easy to compare programs+ fewer numbers to run
− must match a reply to the right opp− one chat, many opp owners

Model B · A number per university

Each university is its own verified number, e.g. Manipal by HeroEd.

+ each chat = one opp, automatically+ university-specific branding
− many numbers to set up & manage− student hears from several numbers− new numbers start with low limits
✓ Our answer — a hybrid, with the CRM in charge

Use a dedicated number for the few big-volume universities (Manipal, Shoolini) where the branding and clean sorting are worth the setup. Use one shared "Counselling by HeroEducation" number for everything else and for students looking at more than one program, where a single relationship simply converts better.

Whichever is used, the CRM's one-contact-many-opportunities model stays in charge — it picks who owns the chat, decides which program a message is about, and caps frequency and consent at the student level across every number, so no one is ever messaged by three HeroEd numbers and a STOP anywhere stops everywhere.

Phase 02 — Foundation

Connect our WABA accounts to DoubleTick

DoubleTick sits on top of the official WhatsApp Business API and becomes our single control layer. Every business number is governed centrally, yet each Learning Consultant still works from their own WhatsApp identity — on laptop and phone.

A Connect every WABA, officially

Each business number — HeroEducation, FMC and others — is connected through the official WhatsApp Business API as a Meta Business Partner. Add numbers as we scale, with no per-seat shared-phone workarounds.

B One centralised inbox for all WABAs combined

Every conversation across every business number lands in a single governed inbox — not one inbox per WABA — so Ops, Marketing and Leadership monitor all accounts in one place. Nothing is lost when an LC is offline, on leave, or mid-handover.

C A managed identity for every LC

Each Learning Consultant gets a business account named as their LC WABA identity — e.g. Devesh HeroEducation — with a display picture of their choosing, so the customer feels a personal relationship while the business retains control.

D Work from anywhere, always in sync

The LC operates from the DoubleTick web portal on a laptop and the DoubleTick app on their phone — the same conversations, agent permissions and SLAs, synced in real time across both.

E Reply from any identity, on the fly

For each conversation the LC decides who the reply comes from — the official WABA the customer first messaged, their own LC identity, or a different account such as Counselling by HeroEducation. They can switch sending account mid-thread to match the context, while the full history stays on one record.

Phase 03 — Intelligent Conversations

Qualify, score and follow up automatically

The engagement layer does the repetitive work — qualifying inbound interest, chasing silent leads on a schedule, and scoring intent — so LCs spend their time only on leads that are ready to convert.

AI Lead Qualification Bot

A no-code bot greets every new lead, asks the qualifying questions an LC would, and only escalates to a human once the lead is worth a human's time. It runs across all entry points and writes its findings straight to the opportunity.

Silence-based follow-ups

Leads rarely reply on the first message. Custom flows detect silence and send a scheduled, escalating sequence of nudges — each after a configurable gap — until the lead re-engages or is marked dormant for a later re-engagement broadcast.

gap = x hours / x minsconfigurable per stageauto-stops on reply

Hot / warm lead scoring

The platform reads intent from how a lead behaves — not just what they say — and translates it into a score and tier that flows straight into the CRM, where we automate the right action for each tier.

Phase 04 — Omnichannel AI

Our AI voice bot & the WhatsApp bot, working as one

Our in-house AI voice bot does both inbound and outbound calls and exchanges webhooks in real time — so it isn't a separate dialer, it's a peer to the WhatsApp bot. The CRM is the conductor; voice and WhatsApp are its two arms, each able to hand a live lead to the other.

How the handoffs work

Each channel covers the other's weakness: voice is high-intent but low-pickup; WhatsApp is low-friction and carries assets but lacks urgency. The handoffs turn that into a single, continuous conversation.

One opportunity, both channels

A single degree-sales journey, shown across the three lanes that run it — so leadership can see exactly where voice leads, where WhatsApp leads, and how the CRM keeps them in step.

Unified scoring & cost-aware routing

Both channels feed one score, and the decision layer picks the cheapest channel that fits the lead's heat — WhatsApp first, voice reserved for hot or high-value leads where its cost is justified.

⇄ The webhook contract (in-house bot)

Because the voice bot is ours, the integration is direct and two-way — no vendor middleware:

CRM/DoubleTick → Voice  POST /call/start — lead phone, program & university identifier, stage, and the latest chat summary so the call opens with full context.

Voice → CRM  POST /call/outcome — transcript, AI summary, sentiment, disposition and next action, written onto the same opportunity timeline as WhatsApp summaries.

CRM → DoubleTick  trigger flow — on a call outcome (missed, asset-requested, follow-up due), fire the matching WhatsApp message.

⚠ One consent surface — not two

Voice carries DND-registry and calling-hour rules; WhatsApp carries opt-in and the 24-hour window. Both must resolve into a single consent state on the opportunity — a STOP on chat should immediately stop the voice bot dialing, and a call opt-out should suppress WhatsApp. Decide this rule before the pilot.

Phase 05 — Lifecycle & Continuity

Beyond the sale — and beyond any one person

The relationship doesn't end at conversion, and it can't depend on a single LC staying forever. These flows carry the customer through post-sale and protect institutional memory through team change.

Post-sales journeys

Once an enrolment is confirmed, automated WhatsApp journeys handle the operational work that usually clogs LC time — collecting documents, chasing fees, and onboarding the student — with the same governed, on-brand identity.

LC offboarding & attrition handling

Sales teams turn over — and today, when an LC leaves, their relationships and chat history often leave with them. With WABA identities governed centrally, an exit becomes a clean, instant handover with zero loss of context.

Conversation summary → CRM activity

Every WhatsApp conversation is summarised by the AI layer and written straight onto the opportunity's activity timeline — with a suggested next step — so the LC sees full chat context inside the CRM without ever switching tabs.

Phase 06 — CRM Integration

Wiring opportunities to conversations

The integration is driven by two CRM events. Opportunity creation opens the conversation and assigns it to the right LC; opportunity updates keep ownership and messaging in lock-step with the lead's journey.

TRIGGER · OPP CREATED

On opportunity creation

From assignment to the first WhatsApp message — fully automated.

Opportunity assigned via HiPrLAP

A new opportunity is created and routed to a Learning Consultant through our existing HiPrLAP assignment logic.

Chat assigns to the LC in DoubleTick

As the opportunity lands with a non-admin user, the corresponding chat in the DoubleTick portal is automatically assigned to that LC.

Webhook fires to DoubleTick

A webhook / API call is triggered to DoubleTick carrying the program and university identifier for the opportunity.

Opening WhatsApp message goes out

Based on that identifier, DoubleTick triggers the correct opening WhatsApp message — the right tone and collateral for the right program.

First touch automated & on-brand
TRIGGER · OPP UPDATED

On opportunity update

Stage change, an LC marking an activity, or any opportunity update.

Update event captured

Any change to the opportunity — a stage change, a logged activity, or an ownership move — fires the update trigger.

Re-assign chat on owner change

If the owner is different from the previous owner, the chat is reassigned to the new owner along with the full historic conversation.

Solves lead clashes & context loss on handover
Stage-aware messaging & collateral

Based on the same program / university identifier, flows deliver the messaging and collateral relevant to the opportunity's current stage.
